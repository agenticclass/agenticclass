# Rajkiran Panuganti

I am Rajkiran Panuganti, founder and CEO of **Cerebro Labs Inc.**, building AgentNava. My research interests include AI agents, reasoning circuits in language models, and multilingual AI reliability.

Previously, I was Head of AI at Krutrim and worked at Microsoft and Google. My Microsoft work included Bing and Microsoft 365 Copilot Chat. I hold a PhD in Computer Science from The Ohio State University and a bachelor's degree from IIT Bombay.

This is my research GitHub account. My engineering profile is [rajkiran-cerebro](https://github.com/rajkiran-cerebro).

## Research and code

- **[CircuitProbe](https://github.com/agenticclass/circuitprobe):** Research on identifying reasoning circuits in transformer models. [Read the paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.00716).
- **[FABRIC](https://github.com/agenticclass/fabric):** A benchmark for AI reliability on Indian financial questions across languages. [Explore the dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/agenticclass/fabric) or [read the paper](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6576883).
- [Publications and research background](https://rajkiranpanuganti.com/research/), including earlier work on parallel computing, compiler optimization, and image super-resolution.
